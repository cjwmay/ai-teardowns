Notion AI: Failure Fallbacks

Structural trust calibration: output is provisional by default, failure degrades to zero; error copy only says "what happened + what you can do".

Day 3

Perplexity：引用标注

Perplexity: Citation Markers

用来源可验证性做 trust calibration：不说"我多确定"，说"从哪来"；无标注=降级信号；claim 级核验。

Calibrating trust through verifiable sources: say "where it's from", not "how sure I am"; missing markers are the degradation signal; claim-level verification.
