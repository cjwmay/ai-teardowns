Perplexity：引用标注

Perplexity: Citation Markers

今天拆什么：Perplexity 回答里的上标引用数字 [1][2] —— 每个有分量的 claim 都附一个可点的来源。

What we're tearing down: the superscript citation numbers [1][2] in Perplexity's answers — every substantive claim comes with a clickable source.

①Trust calibration 做得怎么样？How is trust calibrated?

Perplexity 几乎不用数字置信度。它的 calibration 是可验证性：每个断言配一个 [1]，等于把"信不信我"换成"自己去看来源"—— 产品不说"我有多确定"，而是说"这个信息从哪来"。

  • 成本转移：calibration 从强制确认变成按需核验 —— 你可以信，也可以点开看。对比 Day 2 的 Notion：写文档出错代价高，所以强制确认；读答案的出错代价低，所以做成可选项 —— calibration 的成本应该跟出错的代价成正比，这是第三个证据。
  • 诚实的 tension：引用成立的前提是"编号真的对应支撑这个 claim 的来源"。现实是用户很少点开 —— 标注的存在本身会制造一种"可信感"，哪怕没人验证。提醒：calibration 设计要按"会被跳过"的常态来做，不能假设用户会去验证。

Perplexity barely uses numeric confidence. Its calibration is verifiability: each claim carries a [1], which swaps "trust me" for "go check the source" — the product doesn't say how sure it is, it says where the information comes from.

  • Cost shifting: calibration moves from mandatory confirmation to on-demand verification — you can take the answer at face value, or click through. Compare Day 2's Notion: overwriting a document is costly, so confirmation is mandatory; being misled by an answer is cheaper, so verification is opt-in — the cost of calibration should scale with the cost of being wrong, a third data point for that rule.
  • The honest tension: citations calibrate only if the number genuinely maps to a source that supports the claim. In practice users rarely click — the mere presence of markers manufactures a feeling of reliability even when nothing was verified. A reminder: design calibration for the reality that it will be skipped, not for the assumption that users will verify.

②Graceful degradation 在哪？Where is graceful degradation?

  • 搜不到好来源时：回答变短、来源变少，或者明确说"基于我的训练数据"—— 此时没有引用标注本身就是降级信号（无标注 = 来自记忆，可信度更低）。妙在：降级态不需要新 UI，标注的缺席就是信息。
  • 来源链接失效 / 抓取失败：答案保留，但那条引用变成不可验证 —— 不删答案假装没事，也不编一个假来源。
  • 生成中途失败：半截答案 + 重试，不假装完整。
  • When no good sources are found: answers get shorter, sources fewer, or it explicitly says "based on my training data" — and the absence of citation markers is itself a degradation signal (no marker = from memory, lower trust). The elegant part: the degraded state needs no new UI; the absence of the marker is the message.
  • Dead links / fetch failures: the answer stays, but that citation becomes unverifiable — it neither deletes the answer pretending nothing happened, nor fabricates a fake source.
  • Mid-generation failure: partial answer + retry, no pretending it's complete.

③人有没有 override 的机会？Can the human override?

  • claim 级核验：引用贴在句子/断言级别，人也按句子/断言逐条核验 —— 呼应 Day 1、Day 2 的结论：override 必须和 AI 输出同粒度。别的聊天 UI 只能整体接受/拒绝，Perplexity 把验证粒度切到最细。
  • 成本：一键点开来源；侧边面板让你不离开答案上下文 —— 核验的摩擦力被压到最低。
  • 时机：随时；流式生成中标注就已经可点，不用等答案写完。
  • 追问和"相关问题"是导航式 override：不满意不重来，换个方向问。
  • Claim-level verification: citations attach at the sentence/claim level, and humans verify at that same level — echoing Days 1–2: override must match the AI's output granularity. Other chat UIs only allow accepting/rejecting the whole answer; Perplexity cuts verification to the finest grain.
  • Cost: one click opens the source; the side panel keeps you inside the answer's context — verification friction is pushed to a minimum.
  • Timing: anytime; markers are clickable even while the answer is still streaming.
  • Follow-ups and "related questions" are navigational override: when unsatisfied, don't restart — steer in a new direction.

设计师 takeaway：表达不确定的诚实方式不一定是给数字，也可以是给可验证性 —— 做不出诚实数字时，给 provenance（来源）。给 AI Hub 的迁移：任何带业务分量的 AI 输出，都应该带一个可核验的来源或明确的"无来源"声明；来源的缺席本身就是一条信息。

Designer takeaway: Honesty about uncertainty doesn't have to be a number — it can be verifiability. When honest numeric calibration isn't possible, show provenance instead. For AI Hub: any AI output with business weight should carry a checkable source or an explicit "no source" declaration; the absence of provenance is itself information.

🎙️ 面试怎么讲：Perplexity 的引用标注让我学到一条原则：calibration 不一定要表达"我有多确定"，可以表达"这从哪来" —— 让 AI 的断言粒度和人的核验粒度对齐（claim-level），把验证成本从强制变成按需。我在 AI Hub 里把它变成一条规则：任何带业务分量的 AI 输出，必须附带可核验来源或显式声明"无来源"。

🎙️ How to say it in an interview: Perplexity's citation markers taught me one principle — calibration doesn't have to express "how sure am I", it can express "where did this come from". Align the AI's claim granularity with the human's verification granularity (claim-level), and shift verification from mandatory to on-demand. I turned this into a rule in AI Hub: any AI output carrying business weight must ship with a verifiable source or an explicit "no source" declaration.
