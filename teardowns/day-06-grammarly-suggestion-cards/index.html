Grammarly: Suggestion Cards

今天拆什么：写英文时，Grammarly 用红 / 蓝下划线标出问题；点击后弹出 suggestion card —— 显示建议改法 + 一句 plain-language 解释；Accept 原地替换，Dismiss 忽略。粒度是一个建议一张卡。

What we're tearing down: while writing English, Grammarly marks issues with red / blue underlines; clicking one pops a suggestion card showing the proposed fix plus a one-line plain-language explanation; Accept replaces in place, Dismiss ignores it. The granularity is one card per suggestion.

①Trust calibration 做得怎么样？How is trust calibrated?

它不用数字置信度 —— calibration 是一套颜色分级 + 解释句：红色下划线 = 拼写 / 基础语法（AI 自信度高、改错代价也高，一个词改错整个句意会变）；蓝色 = 清晰度 / 语气 / 风格建议（主观性强、不确定性高）。

  • 取舍：颜色编码偷换了概念 —— 它表达的是问题类型，不是 AI 的确定程度，用户容易误读成"红色 = AI 很确定"。真正的 calibration 发生在卡片里：每个建议都附一句解释（"这里用过去式更准确，因为…"），用户是被理由说服，而不是被分数说服。呼应 Day 1（Copilot 故意不给数字）：当置信度分数本身不可靠时，给理由比给数字更诚实。
  • 弱点：误报（false positive）时下划线照样是红的 —— calibration 失败只能靠用户逐条审查兜底。所以卡片必须带解释：解释是唯一能让用户发现"这个建议是错的"的界面。

No numeric confidence here — calibration is a color hierarchy + explanation line: red underlines = spelling / basic grammar (high AI confidence, but also high cost of a wrong fix — one word can change the whole meaning); blue = clarity / tone / style suggestions (subjective, high uncertainty).

  • The trade-off: color coding swaps the concept — it communicates issue type, not the AI's certainty, and users easily misread it as "red = the AI is very sure". The real calibration happens inside the card: every suggestion carries an explanation ("past tense fits better here because…"), so users are persuaded by the reason, not a score. Echoing Day 1 (Copilot deliberately shows no numbers): when confidence scores are themselves unreliable, giving reasons is more honest than giving numbers.
  • The weakness: on a false positive the underline is red anyway — calibration failure can only be caught by per-suggestion review. That's why the card must carry an explanation: it's the only UI that lets users notice "this suggestion is wrong".

②Graceful degradation 在哪？Where is graceful degradation?

  • 降级分层：AI 建议层挂掉 / 离线 → 退回基础拼写检查（红色波浪线照样在）；建议服务全挂 → "什么都不标"。核心任务（写作）永不断裂，最差情况就是回到没有建议的样子。
  • 不确定时干脆不给建议：只留下一条下划线提示、不弹改法 —— 把"要不要改"的判断权完全留给用户。对比 Day 3（Perplexity 的"无标注 = 降级信号"）：Grammarly 的"有标注、无建议"就是它的降级信号。
  • 降级成"慢"，而不是"错"：长文档 / 网络差时建议延迟出现，而不是一次性全刷出来 —— 宁可慢半拍，也不给半成品建议。
  • Layered degradation: if the AI suggestion layer is down or offline, it falls back to basic spellcheck (the red wavy underlines stay); if suggestions fail entirely, you get "nothing marked". The core task (writing) never breaks — the worst case is just "no suggestions".
  • When uncertain, it offers no suggestion at all: only the underline stays, with no proposed fix — handing the "should I change it" judgment entirely to the user. Contrast with Day 3 (Perplexity's "missing marker = degradation signal"): Grammarly's "marker without suggestion" is its degradation signal.
  • Degrade to "slow", not "wrong": in long documents or on poor networks, suggestions arrive late instead of all at once — better late than half-baked.

③人有没有 override 的机会？Can the human override?

  • 粒度 = 单个 suggestion card：一对一的 Accept（✓）/ Dismiss（×），另有"报告问题"和"加入词典"。Day 1 原则重现 —— override 的粒度与 AI 输出同粒度：AI 给到词，人就按词裁决。
  • 用摩擦换准确性：每条建议必须单独确认，没有"全部接受"一键通关。连续多个建议时成本叠加，人会进入"盲目点通过"模式 —— Grammarly 的对策就是不给你这个捷径，逐条摩擦逼你看一眼。
  • override 本身可逆：接受后还能 Ctrl+Z 撤销，这是第二层保险。呼应 Day 2 的"失败归零"：最差情况是撤销回到改之前，AI 永远留了一条回家的路。
  • Granularity = one suggestion card: one-to-one Accept (✓) / Dismiss (×), plus "report issue" and "add to dictionary". The Day 1 principle returns — override granularity matches output granularity: the AI proposes per word, the human judges per word.
  • Friction in exchange for accuracy: every suggestion must be confirmed individually — there is no "accept all" shortcut. With many consecutive suggestions, costs stack up and people slip into "blindly accept" mode; Grammarly's countermeasure is to deny that shortcut and make the per-item friction force you to look.
  • Overrides are reversible: Ctrl+Z after accepting is a second layer of insurance. Echoing Day 2's "failure degrades to zero": the worst case is undoing back to before the change — the AI always leaves a way home.

设计师 takeaway：建议型 AI 的最佳载体是"贴着原文、带理由、一次只给一条的可审查卡片" —— 让用户在上下文里逐条裁决，而不是侧边栏批量输出全文替换。对 AI Hub 可迁移：SEO Studio 给文案改写建议时，不要一次扔出整段替换，而要行内卡片 + "为什么这么改"解释 + 单条接受，让人用一次点击做仲裁、用撤销做保险。

Designer takeaway: The best carrier for suggestion-style AI is a "reviewable card anchored to the original text, with a reason, one suggestion at a time" — letting users judge item by item in context, not a sidebar dumping whole-paragraph replacements. Transferable to AI Hub: when SEO Studio suggests copy edits, use inline cards with a "why this change" explanation and per-item accept, not one bulk replacement — one click to arbitrate, undo as insurance.

🎙️ 面试怎么讲：Grammarly 不用置信度分数做 trust calibration，而是用"颜色分级 + 每个建议附理由"让用户判断建议值不值得信；override 粒度精确到单个建议、逐条确认、且可撤销 —— 我在 AI Hub 的改写建议里沿用同一模式：行内卡片、逐条裁决、解释即 calibration。

🎙️ How to say it in an interview: Grammarly doesn't calibrate trust with confidence scores — it uses a color hierarchy plus a reason on every suggestion, letting users judge whether it's worth believing; overrides are per-suggestion, confirmed one by one, and reversible. I applied the same pattern to rewrite suggestions in AI Hub: inline cards, per-item arbitration, explanations as calibration.
