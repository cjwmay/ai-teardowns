ChatGPT Canvas：行内改写与 diff

ChatGPT Canvas: Inline Edits & Diffs

今天拆什么：ChatGPT Canvas 里，选中一段文字让 AI 改写（调长度、改语气、加 emoji）—— 改写结果以 diff 高亮直接嵌回文档：新增的绿色、删除的加删除线。你可以逐处接受 / 拒绝，或干脆自己再改。

What we're tearing down: in ChatGPT Canvas, highlight a passage and ask the AI to rewrite it (adjust length, change tone, add emojis) — the rewrite lands back inside the document as an inline diff: additions in green, deletions struck through. Accept or reject per change, or just edit further yourself.

①Trust calibration 做得怎么样？How is trust calibrated?

它的策略是可验证性代替自我评分。AI 从不告诉你"我有 90% 把握"，而是把"我改了什么"摊开成视觉证据 —— diff 本身就是 calibration 界面。

  • 取舍：它把判断成本转嫁给了用户 —— 你得读 diff。但对长文本编辑这笔账是划算的：AI 动的是你的资产（文档），这时透明比省心重要。
  • 对比 Day 1 的 Copilot：ghost text 用"草稿感 + 一次击键"做轻量 calibration；Canvas 用 diff 做重量级 calibration。Calibration 的重量应该和出错的代价成正比（呼应 Day 2 的观点）。

Its strategy is verifiability instead of self-scoring. The AI never says "I'm 90% confident" — it exposes "here's what I changed" as visual evidence, and the diff itself becomes the calibration interface.

  • The trade-off: judgment cost shifts to the user — you must read the diff. For long-form editing that's a fair price: the AI is touching your asset (your document), where transparency beats convenience.
  • Contrast with Day 1's Copilot: ghost text does lightweight calibration ("draft feel + one keystroke"); Canvas does heavyweight calibration with diffs. Calibration weight should scale with the cost of being wrong (echoing Day 2).

②Graceful degradation 在哪？Where is graceful degradation?

  • 失败时归零：改写失败 / 离线，原文纹丝不动 —— 最差情况就是"什么都没发生"，AI 永远搞不坏你的文档。
  • 更关键的是粒度设计：行内改写只动你选中的那几段，而不是整篇重生成。粒度越小，失败的 blast radius 越小 —— 这是 degradation 设计里最被低估的一招：把"一次大失败"切成"多次小失败"。
  • 核心任务（文档本身）永不断裂：AI 永远是编辑器里的一个操作，不是文档存在的前提。
  • Failure degrades to zero: if the rewrite fails or you're offline, the original text is untouched — the worst case is "nothing happened"; the AI can never corrupt your document.
  • The deeper move is granularity: inline edits touch only the passage you selected, never the whole document. Smaller granularity means a smaller blast radius on failure — one of the most underrated degradation tactics: slice one big failure into many small ones.
  • The core task (the document itself) never breaks: AI is an operation inside the editor, never a precondition for the document's existence.

③人有没有 override 的机会？Can the human override?

三层 override，层层递进：

  • ① 选中级改写 —— 只改你圈的那段，不碰全文（输入的粒度由人定）。
  • ② diff 级接受 / 拒绝 —— 逐处点头 / 摇头，粒度与 AI 输出一一对应。
  • ③ 接受后仍可直接手改 + Canvas 的版本历史一键回滚。
  • 关键设计：override 粒度 = 生成粒度 —— AI 改了哪几句，你就能逐句拒绝哪几句，避免了"整篇接受 or 推倒重来"的二元困境（呼应 Day 1 的同粒度原则）。
  • 版本历史是最后的保险：哪怕误点了接受，也能穿越回去。这让"接受"这个动作变得便宜 —— override 成本低，人就敢用 AI。

Three layers of override, each finer than the last:

  • ① Selection-scoped rewrites — only the passage you highlighted is touched (the human sets the input granularity).
  • ② Per-change accept / reject in the diff — granularity matching the AI's output one-to-one.
  • ③ Hand-edit after accepting + Canvas's version history for one-click rollback.
  • The key design: override granularity = generation granularity — whichever sentences the AI changed, you can reject sentence by sentence, escaping the false binary of "accept everything or redo everything" (echoing Day 1's same-granularity principle).
  • Version history is the final insurance: even a mistaken accept can be time-traveled back. That makes "accept" cheap — when overriding is cheap, people dare to use the AI.

设计师 takeaway：diff 是"可验证性即 calibration"的最佳范本。对 AI Hub 可迁移：AI 对用户资产的任何改动，都应该以 diff 形态呈现、接受 / 拒绝粒度与改动粒度一致；版本回滚是 AI 编辑器的标配，不是加分项。

Designer takeaway: Diffs are the best embodiment of "verifiability as calibration". Transferable to AI Hub: any AI change to a user's asset should be presented as a diff, with accept/reject granularity matching the change granularity; version rollback is table stakes for an AI editor, not a bonus.

🎙️ 面试怎么讲：Canvas 不用置信度分数做 trust calibration，而是用 diff 把 AI 的改动变成可验证的视觉证据 —— override 粒度与生成粒度一一对应，版本历史兜底。我在 AI Hub 里沿用同一原则：AI 对用户内容的任何改动都必须 diff 可见、可逐条回滚。

🎙️ How to say it in an interview: Canvas doesn't calibrate trust with confidence scores — it turns the AI's changes into verifiable visual evidence via diffs, with override granularity matching generation granularity one-to-one and version history as the backstop. I applied the same principle in AI Hub: any AI change to user content must be diff-visible and individually reversible.
