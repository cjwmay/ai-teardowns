Linear 的 AI issue 总结：摘要为什么敢被用在决策里

Linear's AI Issue Summaries: Why a Summary Can Be Trusted in Decisions

今天拆什么：Linear 的 issue 堆了几十条评论后，点一下 AI 就在顶部生成一份摘要 —— 但每条结论后面都带着原文锚点，点一下就跳到对应的评论。

What we're tearing down: after a Linear issue accumulates dozens of comments, one click generates an AI summary at the top — but every conclusion carries a source anchor that jumps to the originating comment.

①Trust calibration 做得怎么样？How is trust calibrated?

它的 calibration 策略是"别信我，去看原文" —— 摘要里的每个 claim 都带一个跳转锚点，指向它来自哪条评论。AI 从不自我打分（"我 95% 确定"），而是把溯源链摊在你面前：

  • 这和 Day 3 的 Perplexity 是同一血统：Perplexity 把 calibration 外包给"来源可验证性"；Linear 把它外包给"上下文可回溯性"。共同点是 —— 不说"我多确定"，说"这句话从哪来"。
  • 深层取舍：锚点把"信任"拆成逐条可核验的粒度。摘要敢被用在团队决策里，不是因为它没错，而是因为错的时候你看得出它错在哪一条。校准的成本转移到了用户，但每一条的成本都很小 —— 点一下锚点，一秒验证。
  • 对比 Day 4 的 Canvas diff：diff 让"AI 改了什么"可见，锚点让"AI 压缩掉了什么"可见。压缩是最危险的操作 —— 省略的每一个细节都是一次决策。锚点就是压缩的 calibration。

Its calibration strategy is "don't trust me — check the source". Every claim in the summary carries an anchor jumping to the comment it came from. The AI never self-scores ("I'm 95% sure"); instead it lays out the provenance chain in front of you:

  • Same bloodline as Day 3's Perplexity: Perplexity outsources calibration to "verifiable sources"; Linear outsources it to "traceable context". The shared move — say "where this came from", not "how sure I am".
  • The deeper trade-off: anchors split "trust" into claim-by-claim verifiable units. A summary is safe to use in team decisions not because it's error-free, but because when it's wrong, you can see exactly which line is wrong. The cost of calibration shifts to the user, but each check costs one click and one second.
  • Contrast with Day 4's Canvas diffs: diffs make "what the AI changed" visible; anchors make "what the AI compressed away" visible. Compression is the most dangerous operation — every omitted detail is a decision. Anchors are the calibration of compression.

②Graceful degradation 在哪？Where is graceful degradation?

  • 评论太少时：降级为更短的摘要，而不是硬编。信息不够就不编造 —— 最差情况是"摘要很短"或干脆没有，而不是一条看起来很有说服力的假结论。宁可少说，不瞎说。
  • 结构性设计：摘要永远是 issue 页面上的增强层，不是前提。AI 挂了 / 离线了，评论列表本身照常工作 —— 核心任务（看 issue、回评论）从不断裂。
  • 对比 Day 2 的 Notion AI：失败归零（"什么都没发生"）。Linear 同理 —— 没有摘要，你只是回到手动读评论的状态，没有被"推向"一个错误的结论。
  • When there are too few comments: it degrades to a shorter summary instead of inventing one. Insufficient information means no fabrication — the worst case is "a short summary" or none at all, never a persuasive-looking fake conclusion. Say less rather than make things up.
  • Structural design: the summary is always an enhancement layer on the issue page, never a precondition. If the AI is down or offline, the comment list works as usual — the core task (reading and replying) never breaks.
  • Echoing Day 2's Notion AI: failure degrades to zero ("nothing happened"). Same here — without a summary, you're simply back to reading comments manually; you're never pushed toward a wrong conclusion.

③人有没有 override 的机会？Can the human override?

  • 就地编辑：摘要生成后可以直接改 —— override 的粒度是整份摘要，改完它就变成了团队的资产，不再是"AI 的输出"。这是微妙但关键的一步：被编辑过的摘要，信任的归属发生了转移（从 AI 转移到人）。
  • 重新生成（regenerate）：对摘要不满意，一键重跑 —— 和评论列表的实时性对齐（新评论进来，摘要可以 refresh）。override 的时机是"每次信息更新"，而不是一次性。
  • 对比 Day 4 的 Canvas（逐条接受 / 拒绝 + 版本回滚）：Linear 的 override 更粗糙 —— 只有"整份重写"级别。但它的聪明之处是把评论列表本身当作终极 override：对摘要的任何怀疑，答案都在下面 30 条评论里躺着。这不是真正的 override 控件，而是"可验证性即 override"的又一个实例 —— 你不需要一个按钮去推翻它，因为你始终可以越过它去看原文。
  • Edit in place: the summary can be edited directly after generation — override granularity is the whole summary, and once edited it becomes the team's asset, not "the AI's output". A subtle but crucial shift: an edited summary transfers the ownership of trust (from AI to human).
  • Regenerate: unhappy with the summary, re-run with one click — aligned with the comment list's real-time nature (new comments arrive, the summary can refresh). Override timing is "every information update", not one-and-done.
  • Contrast with Day 4's Canvas (per-change accept / reject + version rollback): Linear's override is coarser — only "rewrite the whole thing". But its clever move is treating the comment list itself as the ultimate override: any doubt about the summary is answerable by the 30 comments lying underneath. That's not a real override control — it's another instance of "verifiability as override": you never need a button to overturn it, because you can always go past it to the source.

设计师 takeaway：摘要是最危险的 AI 输出形态 —— 压缩必然伴随取舍，而取舍必然影响决策。所以规则是：逐点可溯源的摘要是 calibration 界面，无法溯源的摘要是信任泄漏。可迁移到 AI Hub：AI 做的任何"压缩"（总结用户反馈、提炼 SEO insight）都必须给每个结论留一条回证据的路；摘要必须能就地编辑、一键重跑 —— 摘要被编辑的那一刻，它就从"AI 的输出"变成"人的资产"。

Designer takeaway: Summarization is the most dangerous AI output form — compression always entails trade-offs, and trade-offs always affect decisions. The rule: a summary that is traceable point-by-point is a calibration interface; an untraceable one is a trust leak. Transferable to AI Hub: any AI "compression" (summarizing user feedback, distilling SEO insights) must give every conclusion a path back to evidence; the summary must be editable in place and re-runnable — the moment a summary is edited, it stops being "the AI's output" and becomes "the human's asset".

🎙️ 面试怎么讲：Linear 的 AI 总结之所以敢被用在团队决策里，是因为它不做置信度评分 —— 它给每个结论配了原文锚点，错的时候你看得出它错在哪一条。摘要是最危险的 AI 输出，因为压缩即决策。我在 AI Hub 里会坚持同一原则：AI 做的任何压缩都要逐点可溯源，摘要必须能就地编辑和重跑 —— 被编辑过的摘要，信任的归属就从 AI 转到了人。

🎙️ How to say it in an interview: Linear's AI summary earns a place in team decisions because it skips confidence scores — every conclusion carries a source anchor, so when it's wrong you can see exactly which line is wrong. Summarization is the most dangerous AI output form, because compression is decision-making. I hold the same line in AI Hub: any AI compression must be traceable point-by-point, and the summary must be editable in place and re-runnable — once a summary is edited, the ownership of trust transfers from the AI to the human.
