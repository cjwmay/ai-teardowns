Arc Search "Browse for me"：agentic 流程的进度表达与中断机会

Arc Search "Browse for me": Progress Display & Interruption in Agentic Flows

今天拆什么：你输入一个问题，Arc 替你去读约 6 个网页，然后当场生成一页为你定制的答案页。拆的是这个 agentic 流程本身 —— 它运行时会把"正在读哪些网站"一条条列出来给你看，但你看得到、干预不了。

What we're tearing down: you type a question, and Arc reads roughly six web pages on your behalf, then generates a custom answer page on the spot. We're dissecting the agentic process itself — it lists "which sites I'm reading" live, so you can watch but not steer.

①Trust calibration 做得怎么样？How is trust calibrated?

它把 calibration 从"结果"前移到"过程"：运行时实时展示正在读哪些网站（逐条勾掉），等于向你报账"我看了什么"。域名本身就是可信度代理（credibility proxy）—— 看到权威媒体你会更信，看到内容农场你会打折。UI 不用说"我很确定"，而是让你看到证据链。

  • 成页后切换到第二套机制：每节附来源链接 + 底部的 "Dive Deeper" 区 —— 从过程透明转到结果可验证。
  • 取舍：过程展示买的是耐心（perceived wait）—— 几秒的等待变得可忍受。但它的 calibration 是滞后的：中途你能看到它在读，却无法判断读得对不对、取舍合不合理，质量只能等成页后评判。

It moves calibration from "the result" to "the process": while running, it shows which sites it's reading, ticking them off one by one — an itemized account of "what I looked at". The domain itself is a credibility proxy: reputable outlets raise your trust, content farms discount it. The UI never says "I'm confident"; it shows you the evidence chain.

  • After the page is built, a second mechanism kicks in: source links per section plus the "Dive Deeper" section at the bottom — from process transparency to result verifiability.
  • The trade-off: the progress display buys patience (perceived wait) — a few seconds of waiting become tolerable. But its calibration is lagging: mid-flight you can see what it reads, but can't judge whether it read well or chose well; quality can only be assessed once the page is done.

②Graceful degradation 在哪？Where is graceful degradation?

藏在"读到几个"里 —— 降级是质量梯度，不是二元成败：

  • 某个网站打不开 / 读取失败：直接跳过，用已读到的继续合成。读到 6 个和只读到 3 个，页面完整度不同，但页面照样生成。
  • 这是多源 agentic 任务的天然优势：单步生成要么成要么败，多步任务天然带部分成功（partial success）状态。
  • 核心任务（回答问题）不断裂；最差情况是信息少一点的页面，不是空白报错。页面式输出本身也是降级载体 —— 哪怕合成质量打折，骨架（标题、分节、链接）还保留了浏览价值。

It's hidden in "how many it managed to read" — degradation is a quality gradient, not a binary:

  • If a site fails to load or parse, it's skipped and synthesis continues with what was read. Six sources vs. three means different completeness, but the page is still generated either way.
  • This is the natural advantage of multi-source agentic tasks: single-shot generation either succeeds or fails, while multi-step tasks naturally carry a partial success state.
  • The core task (answering the question) never breaks; the worst case is a thinner page, never a blank error. The page-format output is itself a degradation vessel — even if synthesis quality dips, the skeleton (headings, sections, links) retains browsing value.

③人有没有 override 的机会？Can the human override?

  • 中途中断：粒度最粗 —— 只有"放弃 / 重跑"，你不能在中间说"别读这个，换那个"。Agent 的过程是可看不可改的黑盒。
  • 真正的 override 点在输入和输出两端：输入端改 query 重跑（代价 = 重来一遍的几秒钟）；输出端逐个点开来源链接自己验证，或退回传统搜索自己看（Arc 本来就保留了传统搜索入口）。
  • 设计洞见：agentic 流程的 interrupt 是个连续谱 —— Arc 选了 run-to-completion + 全过程可视化，而不是中途决策检查点（"找到这些来源，继续还是改方向？"）。对 5 秒的任务这个选择是对的；但对 5 分钟的 research agent，缺检查点就是设计债。
  • Mid-flight interruption: the coarsest granularity — only "abandon / re-run"; you can't say "skip that one, read this instead" in the middle. The agent's process is a watch-only black box.
  • The real override points sit at both ends: refine the query and re-run (cost = a few seconds of redo), or verify by opening source links one by one afterward, or fall back to classic search (which Arc deliberately keeps as an entry point).
  • The design insight: interruption in agentic flows is a spectrum — Arc chose run-to-completion plus full process visibility instead of mid-flight decision checkpoints ("found these sources — continue or redirect?"). Right call for a 5-second task; but for a 5-minute research agent, the missing checkpoint is design debt.

设计师 takeaway：在 agentic 流程里，进度展示买的是耐心（perceived performance），来源展示买的是信任。但"可看不可改"的过程是半成品 —— 长时间的 agent 任务，需要在关键决策点设检查点，而不是只给人"全盘重来"的选项。

Designer takeaway: in agentic flows, progress display buys patience (perceived performance) and source display buys trust. But a watch-only process is half-finished design — long-running agents need checkpoints at key decision points, not just an "all-or-nothing re-run" for the human.

🎙️ 面试怎么讲：Arc 的 Browse for me 把 trust calibration 做在过程里 —— 实时列出它在读哪几个网站，让人用域名判断可信度，而不是等一个置信度数字。我在 AI Hub 的多步 AI 流程里借用了这个思路：每一步都展示输入来源，让人能中途质疑，而不是只能接受最终结果。

🎙️ How to say it in an interview: Arc's Browse for me calibrates trust inside the process — it lists which sites it's reading in real time, letting users judge credibility by domain instead of waiting for a confidence score. I borrowed this in AI Hub's multi-step AI flows: every step shows its input sources, so people can question mid-flight instead of only accepting the final result.
